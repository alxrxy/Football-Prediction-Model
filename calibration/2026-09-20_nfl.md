# NFL 2026 week 2 — Sunday slate, 2026-09-20

14 Sunday games graded. TNF (DET @ BUF, 2026-09-17) is graded but outside this report's slate window; it is
included in the week-2 totals below. MNF (NYG @ LA, 2026-09-21) gets its own report once graded.

## Key finding: the baseline's edges got worse as its disagreement with the market grew

| baseline |edge| | ATS (decided games; pushes excluded) |
|---|---|
| any | 5-9 (+1 push) |
| >= 2 pts | **2-8** |
| >= 3 pts | 2-7 |
| >= 4 pts | **2-6** |
| >= 6 pts | 1-2 (+1 push) |

The same records hold for the Sunday slate alone and the full week, because TNF's edge was 1.0. The more the
baseline disagreed with the line, the more often it was wrong: at 0-2 points it went 2-1, and above 2 points it
won only PIT @ NE and SEA @ ARI. The directional diagnostics below say the same thing. The best-fit weight on the
edge is **w = -0.68** and corr(edge, cover residual) is **-0.325**, so this week the edge pointed the wrong way.
**This is the second week running:** week 1 had w = -0.64 and corr -0.222 (`2026-09-13_nfl.md`). ML's larger
edges went 2-3 at >= 2 and 0-1 at >= 4, too few to say whether it shares the pattern.

This is 10 decided games on one side and 13 in week 1. It is not yet evidence the baseline's edges are
anti-predictive; roughly two weeks of negative w can happen by chance. It **is** a clear reason not to act on large
baseline disagreements until the pattern is tested over more weeks. "Disagreement is where the value is" is
exactly the assumption the value flag is built on.

## WARNING — CLV from this week is corrupted by P34; exclude the affected games

**Do not use the week-2 CLV totals the grader prints without excluding CLE @ TB and CIN @ HOU.** On 2026-09-20
ESPN's pickcenter was blocked, so every week-2 close came from the fallback (`oddsapi_last_pregame`). That fallback
takes the median of American odds across books, and prices straddling -100/+100 produce impossible values
(P34, calibration-log 2026-09-20, diagnosed and unfixed). Four rows are corrupted:

| game | model | flagged | stored close price (home / away) | stored CLV |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CLE @ TB | baseline | **yes** | -2 / -118 | -44.9 pp |
| CLE @ TB | ML | no | -2 / -118 | +44.9 pp |
| CIN @ HOU | baseline | no | -116 / -1 | +46.0 pp |
| CIN @ HOU | ML | no | -116 / -1 | -46.0 pp |

| CLV, week 2 | as stored | excluding the 4 rows |
|---|---|---|
| baseline, all leans | n=15, mean +0.02 pp, **sd 17.18** | n=13, mean **-0.05 pp**, sd 0.29, beat close 5/13 (5 no move) |
| baseline, flagged | n=2, mean **-22.34 pp** | n=1 (PHI @ TEN), **+0.24 pp** |
| ML, all leans | n=15, mean -0.15 pp, sd 17.18 | n=13, mean **-0.09 pp**, sd 0.52, beat close 5/13 (4 no move) |

The uncorrupted rows are also weaker than their label: every close this week is our own snapshot from about
72 minutes before kickoff, not a true closing line. Closing lines can't be recovered once the games are over. Any
multi-week CLV total that includes week 2 must drop these four rows until P34 is fixed and they are recomputed.
